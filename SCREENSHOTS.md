# Screenshots

All 4 screenshots are real renders of this build, taken in headless Chromium at 1280x800, each under the 256 KiB cap.

- `01-start.png`: the start screen, LEONIDA HEAT: SIXTH CITY, with the story and the START NEW GAME and CONTINUE SAVED GAME buttons.
- `02-driving.png`: driving a stolen car near The Strip, with traffic, the HUD, and the minimap.
- `03-mission.png`: a mission in progress, on foot next to a car to steal, with the mission box and the waypoint marker.
- `04-city.png`: on foot in the city, showing the streets, buildings, and pedestrians.
